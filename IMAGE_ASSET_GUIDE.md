## Replacement rules

1. Use only approved Nikoo Homes 9 collateral—never imagery from another Nikoo phase.
2. Remove all third-party watermarks and partner branding at the source; do not retouch a watermark out of someone else’s file.
3. Keep plan labels, dimensions, north arrows and legends readable on a 390 px-wide phone.
4. If an official architectural render replaces a conceptual render, remove the on-image conceptual disclaimer only after confirming written approval.
5. Preserve the mobile hero as a separately composed portrait export; do not rely on a center crop of the desktop banner.
